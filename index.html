About the Conference

World Diabetes Day 2026 – Diabetes in Focus is an internal medicine scientific conference taking place on 15 November 2026 at Semiramis InterContinental Cairo.

The conference brings together healthcare professionals to discuss developments in diabetes prevention, treatment, and the management of complications. The scientific program explores cardiovascular and kidney health, obesity, emerging therapies, and practical approaches to patient care.

Through expert presentations and scientific discussions, the conference aims to support knowledge sharing and better outcomes for people living with diabetes.
